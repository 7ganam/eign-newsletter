# Lead research instructions

For lead discovery, qualification, strategy creation, or a daily lead-research run, read [the lead research pipeline guide](docs/lead-research-pipeline.md) before doing any work. The linked Notion guide and the live Notion databases are the source of truth; local lead JSON is legacy context only unless the user explicitly brings it back into scope.

When creating or reformatting EIGN Notion lead pages, use the repository-local [eign-notion-lead-pages skill](.codex/skills/eign-notion-lead-pages/SKILL.md). Read its bundled page standard and template before drafting or publishing. It can also be invoked explicitly as `$eign-notion-lead-pages`.

Use the native Notion connector. Keep the user's links-first page layout, preserve source evidence and uncertain qualification, and retain live database properties and user edits. Layout work does not authorize changing lead data, outreach, or unrelated pages.

All lead work is `RESEARCH ONLY · NO OUTREACH` unless the user separately and explicitly authorizes outreach. Do not send messages, collect private contact details, or change contact/follow-up fields during a research run.
