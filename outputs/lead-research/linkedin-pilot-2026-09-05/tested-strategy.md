# Saudi pre-build buyer research: tested LinkedIn strategy

Tested on 5 September 2026 in the user's logged-in Microsoft Edge LinkedIn account. This is a research-only pilot, not an outreach experiment or a replacement for the canonical lead list.

## Decision

Keep ordinary LinkedIn for now. Start with explicit project-request posts, use current pre-incubation admissions to discover quieter founders, and verify the exact person-project pair before qualification. Newsletter reading belongs mainly in the context/resource lane.

The pilot found real development requests and fresh projects, but **zero prospects whose current unbuilt core product and complete software-team maximum of two were both established**. It validates useful discovery techniques, not a guaranteed supply of qualified buyers or commercial conversion.

## What was actually tested

- Nine distinct post queries, eleven query/filter/sort passes and 59 visible top-level post impressions reviewed. These are not 59 unique people, projects or leads; repeated search appearances count again.
- Two user-seed newsletter editions read in full with visible comment threads: The Saudi Landing and Building Between Markets. Supporting agents separately checked public posts, programs, a further newsletter and paid-product documentation.
- Exact-name/profile checks for promising authors, project history, original posts and visible collaborators. No bulk profile traversal.
- Free Posts filters worked: Latest, posting date and author/member/company controls were visible. No commercial-search restriction appeared during the pilot; future limits are not ruled out.
- No outreach, reactions, follows, connections, saves, subscriptions, contact enrichment or purchases. Canonical app/data files were not changed.

This was a bounded, non-random sample. Rankings are personalized and queries had different date windows; counts cannot be interpreted as statistical comparisons of whole channels.

## The operating strategy

### A. Start with demand, not a founder title

Allocate an initial 60% of discovery time to explicit requests, 30% to fresh cohort/project introductions and 10% to newsletters/context. These are proposed starting allocations, not measured optimal percentages.

Use the tested Arabic request queries above in Posts, sorted Latest. Rotate individual phrases instead of one giant Boolean expression. Recognize contract language such as project assignment, a brief, deliverables, timeline and vendor requirements. Distinguish three compensation modes: paid project, employee recruitment, equity-only cofounder.

Do not blanket-exclude every job-related phrase: a legitimate project contract can use a job-opportunity hashtag. The complete sentence must determine intent.

Suggested next tests, **not yet validated in this pilot**: `"نبحث عن شركة" "تطوير"`, `"تطبيق" "بنظام المشروع"`, `"looking for" "MVP" "Saudi"`, and `"فكرة تطبيق" "مبرمج"`. Check Saudi relevance after identifying the project; the poster may omit Saudi from the text.

### B. Catch founders before graduation and funding publicity

Use an exact, current edition such as Misk Launchpad 10.0; prioritize admission, ideation and founder-introduction posts. Find names through the announcement, then inspect each person's own project evidence. Repeat this pattern for other official pre-incubation programs only after confirming the current edition and its stage.

Do not qualify a person because they joined Misk, Antler, a no-code workshop, or a startup newsletter. These channels contain built products, technical founders, advisers and people seeking equity partners.

### C. Apply two independent checks before scoring

1. **Persona eligibility:** identified person and project; concrete Saudi-market basis; owner or authorized commissioning role; first core software product still unbuilt; no established engineering team; supported maximum of two hands-on software builders, including relevant contractors/cofounders.
2. **Commercial intent:** evidence of purchasing external development, suitable project scope, provider eligibility, timing and payment readiness. Keep amount unknown unless actually stated. Funding sought, accelerator admission and fundraising are not a development budget.

Both checks must pass for a fully qualified buyer. A high intent score cannot compensate for an existing app or an established team. A perfect-looking early founder with no evidence of paid demand is an early-project watchlist entry.

Classify product status explicitly: idea, nonfunctional mockup, functioning prototype, private beta, shipped core product, unknown. Do not relabel a functioning beta as unbuilt merely because launch is pending. A marketing landing page alone is not the core app; an absent app-store result is not proof of no app.

### D. Keep uncertain research useful without inflating the lead list

Maintain three clearly separate outputs: qualified prospects, research holds with one decisive missing fact, and excluded/adjacent examples. No holds should appear as qualified just to meet a target of 50.

Each record needs the original post URL, canonical person URL, separate project identity, observed date, publication date/label, product evidence, builder-count lower and upper bounds, authority, vendor intent, source-control group and exact next check. Root and agent readings of the same post are one origin; profile plus authored post are not two independent publishers.

Team upper bounds are the hardest public-only fact. A statement that the founder is the sole builder, or a credible complete current roster, is stronger than finding only one developer on LinkedIn. If no public evidence establishes the maximum, stop with an explicit hold; do not invent certainty. Any later direct verification would require a separate outreach instruction.

### E. Work in short measurable passes

Proposed schedule: two 60-minute research sessions per week, not an automation created by this task. Each session: 25 minutes explicit-request discovery, 15 minutes current-cohort discovery, 15 minutes checking the most promising unique projects, five minutes recording results and source yield. Prioritize verification over more browsing if a clear candidate appears.

Prioritize recent requests; put older posts in revalidation. Suggested review windows: current requests within seven days, recent cohort holds within fourteen days, low-yield context sources monthly. These are workflow defaults, not claims about sales cycles.

Track unique projects, explicit paid briefs, persona passes, missing-team cases, duplicates, exclusions, and qualified prospects per research hour. Track discovery time separately from verification time. After two consecutive source passes add under 10% new evidence-supported candidates, change/deprioritize that source and report shortfall.

## Newsletters: keep, but change their job

[The Saudi Landing](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/7492136610118098945/) and [Building Between Markets](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/7495425255386230785/) supplied market context, not qualified buyers in the two editions inspected. This small sample does not establish that future editions cannot yield leads. Check future named project stories or first-person comments, not subscriber lists or generic agreement. Program posts and specific founder admissions had more useful discovery information in this pilot.

## Paid services decision

**Buy nothing yet.** The main missing facts were project ownership, current product stage, team capacity and paid-vendor intent, not access to enough names.

- **Sales Navigator Core:** consider a short trial only if repeated free sessions reveal a real search restriction or substantial time lost to unavailable combined targeting filters. LinkedIn advertises advanced search and a starting price of US$119.99/month. Local currency, tax, checkout amount and trial eligibility were not verified. Neither headcount filtering nor recent activity proves an unbuilt app or a complete <=2-builder roster. [Official plan comparison](https://business.linkedin.com/sell/sales-navigator/compare-plans?lang=en)
- **Premium Business:** consider only if ordinary people/company search limits become the main obstacle and existing targeting is otherwise sufficient. It advertises unlimited search, but is not a solution to missing project evidence. [Official product page](https://premium.linkedin.com/small-business/business)
- **Sales Navigator Advanced, Apollo, Crunchbase and MAGNiTT:** no demonstrated substantial incremental benefit for this strict research-only persona in this pilot. Do not buy outreach/enrichment or funding-database features to solve an intent/qualification problem. Capabilities and official sources are recorded in `paid-tools.json`; paid account results were not tested.

If a paid trial becomes justified, compare equivalent free and paid research batches. Proposed success criterion: at least 30% less discovery time per unique evidence-supported candidate, without worsening downstream qualification. Confirm improved qualified yield before committing annually; no tool's total database size is an adequate success metric.

## Artifacts and limitations

`live-pilot.json` contains the signed-in query/pass log, three core research holds, an adjacent procurement example, negative controls and newsletter checks. `public-candidates.json`, `community-channels.json` and `paid-tools.json` preserve parallel public research. Records overlap across files and must not be added together as distinct leads.

This pilot does not update `/lead-research`, does not claim current qualification for the old 50-lead dataset, and does not establish that public-only research can satisfy every team/ownership gate for every prospect. It supplies an evidence-backed next strategy and prevents the original existing-app/team false-positive from being repeated.
