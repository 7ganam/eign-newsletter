# Saudi pre-build buyer research — revised plan

Revision: proposed v2, reviewed 2026-09-05. Scope: research only. This review creates a proposed replacement classification and strategy; it does not change the live app, canonical records, archives, or existing user edits.

## Executive Summary

Review of all 50 current records recommends 38 exclusions, 5 candidates to investigate first, and 7 other holds. None currently has enough evidence to confirm every revised gate. These are judgments about the recorded evidence, with selected live checks; they are not 50 freshly verified external profiles. The full audit preserves every original ID, score, evidence link, reason and next check.

The central correction is to qualify the specific first software build. Product maturity and team capacity must pass before a lead receives a commercial-priority band. Funding, a recent release or a founder title cannot compensate for an already-built product.

## 1. The revised persona

A decision-maker with a specific Saudi-market first software project, whose intended core product is still unbuilt, with at most two current software builders and an unresolved delivery need. Mobile apps remain the priority; relevant web platforms and internal systems remain eligible. The known customer Abdulrahman remains a reference, never a prospect count.

- **Core product:** idea, scoped brief, design prototype or manual pilot can qualify. A functioning app/platform serving the intended core use case is out of scope, even when private or built with no-code tools. A brochure website or standard business tool alone does not disqualify an SME's first custom product. Ambiguous private alpha remains on hold.
- **Software team:** count active hands-on technical co-founders, employees and known contractors. Zero, one or two can qualify. Three confirmed builders fails the gate. Record advisers separately. A signed delivery provider may mean the build need is already covered, even with no internal engineers.
- **Evidence of the maximum:** a complete, recent founder/team statement or project roster, cross-checked against public roles, can support a ceiling of two. Seeing two engineers on LinkedIn establishes only a visible minimum. Missing profiles never prove an empty team.
- **Saudi relevance:** require actual documented operations or a specific current Saudi launch initiative. Separate a concrete future launch from an unconfirmed expansion wish. Do not infer nationality or market activity from names or attendance alone.
- **Authority and intent:** verify the current person/project match, decision role, proposed functionality and active build initiative. Entrepreneurship interest, a job title or generic digital-transformation language is insufficient.
- **Behaviour:** retain only observed professional activity and business topics. Do not infer psychological traits, wealth, religion, politics, health or risk appetite.

## 2. Separate fit from buying readiness

Maintain independent outputs:

1. `persona_fit`: confirmed, hold, or out of scope, with a reason for every gate.
2. `delivery_intent`: paid external build, exploring external help, employee hiring, equity-only co-founder, unknown, or already covered.
3. `budget`: explicit allocation, relevant available funds, plausible operating capacity, unknown, or explicitly unavailable.
4. `research_action`: the exact missing fact and best source to check next.

An equity-only co-founder search can reveal a pre-build project without showing a paid development opportunity. Unknown budget retains a fit-confirmed research candidate but earns no budget points. Program applications, mentorship and technical credits do not establish development cash.

After all fit gates pass, rank specific build commitment (0–6), paid external-delivery intent (0–6), available project funds (0–4), and timing (0–4). Proposed A requires at least 15, explicit paid external intent, relevant available funds, a real trigger within 90 days and a second review. B is 10–14, or a higher total missing an A prerequisite. Lower-scoring fits remain research/nurture. These are operational priorities, not purchase probabilities. Freeze rule IDs and calibrate before scaling; preserve v1 scores as history.

## 4. Refocus discovery and reusable resources

Use three lanes without fixed result quotas:

1. **First-product founders:** recent public admissions to idea-validation/pre-incubation programs and founder descriptions of planned MVPs.
2. **Pre-company founders:** specific public product concepts, prototype plans and technical-partner searches, separated by cash versus equity/hiring intent.
3. **Operating non-software SMEs:** owner-authored plans to replace a manual workflow or launch a first custom product. Existing customers and business revenue are context; a specific build need is still required. This lane is a hypothesis to test, not a proven higher-converting segment.

Resource changes:

- Prioritize public admission and Ideate posts for [Misk Launchpad](https://hub.misk.org.sa/en/programs/entrepreneurship/misk-launchpad/). Its current 10.0 page lists a 6 September 2026 start. The program also admits MVP/early-launch teams, so participation alone cannot qualify someone. A named participant roster is not guaranteed.
- Split [The Garage](https://thegarage.sa/) into incubator, accelerator and hosted programs; check exact stage criteria. Add [Antler Riyadh residency](https://www.antler.co/location/menap) admissions as a pilot, with separate checks for internal/co-founder building and paid external demand.
- Separate Startup Unlocked from [Sanabil Accelerator](https://mena.500.co/founders/mena/seed-accelerator), whose explicit criteria include an MVP and early traction. Use the latter mainly to check product maturity.
- Version StartSmart by edition. Its [tenth edition](https://startsmartsaudi.com/programs/competitions?page=1) excludes idea-only applicants, requires at least SAR 5,000 revenue and offers in-kind technical benefits. Historical idea-stage criteria and cash assumptions must not be transferred into it. Manual-service revenue still warrants candidate-specific examination.
- Treat funding stories, VC portfolios, demo days and app-launch newsletters as verification sources. Only explicit idea-stage event tracks become acquisition sources.
- Verify current MVP Lab terms before treating support as cash available to hire a vendor. The official page resolved but provided no readable body in this review.

Proposed search phrases, not claimed results: `"أبحث عن شركة برمجة" السعودية`, `"تطوير تطبيق لمشروعي"`, `"نبحث عن شريك تقني" الرياض`, `"MVP development partner" Saudi`, `"building our first app" Riyadh`. Record whether each result seeks an agency, employee, equity co-founder or something unclear. Discover externally, then use free LinkedIn for exact-name/current-role checks in small read-only passes. No extensions or automated profile traversal.

Every resource needs an exact program/edition/phase, public harvesting recipe, access status, priority, cadence, caveats and source family. Preserve every user seed and its useful/low-signal/duplicate/rejected classification. Do not count website and official social feed as two independent organizations.

## 5. Fast, quality-controlled collection workflow

1. Preserve v1 and add a v2 assessment layer; keep researcher notes, archives and overrides. Fix eligibility generation before importing another batch.
2. Calibrate on ten examples spanning obvious exclusion, apparent fit and boundary cases. Include STOQA, a functional private alpha, a complete two-person technical roster, an incomplete roster, an equity-only request and a manual SME brief. Review the reasons, not just totals.
3. Run three independent discovery streams: current pre-incubation admissions; specific public first-build requests; non-software SME owner projects. Each researcher owns a separate output file. The coordinator owns canonical merge and provenance.
4. Screen in a cheap sequence: identity/project and Saudi basis; existing product; team capacity; commercial intent/budget. Stop costly enrichment once a decisive disqualifier is found. Keep ambiguous cases visibly held.
5. Run a second parallel verification pass only on unresolved facts for plausible candidates. Recheck consequential launch/team/funding claims directly; preserve contradictions. Deduplicate by canonical person and verified project identity, merging compatible evidence rather than selecting the highest score.
6. Publish valid partial batches. Fifty remains a research target, not a validation requirement. Remove fixed 30/10/10 lead and 25-source quotas from correctness tests.
7. Track each source pass's unique reviewed candidates, confirmed fits, commercially ready fits, holds, exclusions and research minutes. If two consecutive completed 20-candidate passes produce fewer than two new confirmed fits each, pause that channel and redirect effort. If remaining viable channels are exhausted, report the shortfall; do not weaken gates.

Use actual event dates separately from observation dates. Proposed review intervals: 30 days for product/team claims, 14 days for active vendor requests and 90 days for funding facts; expire sooner at a stated milestone. Unknown event dates earn no recency points. Revisiting an old page does not refresh the build event.

## 6. Workspace and validation enhancements

- Display core-product status, visible builder minimum, supported builder maximum, team-audit coverage, paid-build intent, budget evidence, fit result and next research action. Keep calculated fields, provenance and evidence read-only; preserve editable research notes and review controls.
- Retain the centered evidence modal. Group evidence by the claim it supports or contradicts, showing event/observed dates, publisher/control group and access limitations.
- Replace hardcoded verified gates and blanket high confidence with claim-backed evaluation. Eliminate substring lifecycle inference (`pre-MVP` must not become `launched`), invented fallback references and automatic manual-review stamps.
- Validate real calendar dates, claim relevance, evidence coverage, source control groups, contradictions and semantic scoring rules. An original job post and its mirror share one origin; two genuinely independent sources can share a source type.
- Compute source discovery yield from unique v2-eligible project IDs; distinguish discovery from corroboration. Preserve v1 yields as historical values and use null for an unmeasured v2 yield.
- Add regressions for a live app with a high score, incomplete zero-engineer roster, complete two-builder roster, third engineer, assigned agency, private alpha, equity-only request, noncash award, undated trigger and valid partial dataset. Confirm notes/archives survive regeneration and existing tables stay untouched.

## Caveats and remaining questions

Public research may never establish a reliable whole-team ceiling for some prospects. Keep those records unresolved; research-only scope does not permit contacting founders to fill the gap. No calls, messages, follows, reactions, saves, content, contact-detail enrichment or outreach tracking are included.

Remaining research questions: Which channel yields the most confirmed pre-build fits per hour? How often do fit-confirmed founders actually seek paid external delivery? Do manual-SME briefs offer better cash readiness? Which programs assign their own providers? These require measured pilot batches; this review makes no conversion-rate claim.
