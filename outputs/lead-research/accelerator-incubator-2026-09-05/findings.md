### What this trial changed

- **Discovery works; sales qualification remains unproven.** Do not describe the three records as verified outsourced-development buyers.
- The strongest new candidate came from The Garage's early challenge pathway, followed by a current venture-site check. Challenge participation, membership and incubator admission must remain distinct.
- Current [Misk Launchpad](https://hub.misk.org.sa/en/programs/misk-launchpad/) material lists September 6, 2026–January 2, 2027. Phase-one acceptance is conditional progression, not full admission or cash. Fresh selection posts also surfaced already-built products such as AIDE AI and ClarifyAi.
- [Antler's official cohort calendar](https://www.antler.co/cohort-start-dates) lists Riyadh October 11, 2026. Admission and possible investment do not establish cash received. The inspected searches mostly repeated Faisal Abduljawad and Adilet Zakiyev, already in the database; no new records or provenance were assigned to those duplicates.
- [The Garage program distinction](https://thegarage.sa/garage-programs) remains important: incubator/prototype path is useful; scale-up accelerator participants are often too mature.
- Next manual research review: verify Momawwil's current core functionality, complete builder capacity, paid vendor openness and build deadline before expanding the name count. No monitoring automation or outreach was started.
